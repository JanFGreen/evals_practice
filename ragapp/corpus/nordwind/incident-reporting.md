# Reporting accidents and incidents
Version: 2.0 | Valid from: 2024-01-15 | Status: current

## Emergencies
If life or health is at risk, first call the emergency number 112.
Then notify the health and safety duty desk on internal extension 7000 (24/7).

## Workplace accidents
You report every workplace accident, including a minor injury, within 24 hours using the form in the SafeDesk system, and you inform your direct manager.
In the report give the date and time, the location (e.g. hall A, B or C), a description of the event, the type of injury and the details of witnesses.

## Dangerous occurrences
A dangerous occurrence is a situation in which nobody was hurt but an accident could have happened (e.g. a falling pallet, oil spilled in an aisle).
You report such events in the SafeDesk system within 72 hours. Reports may be anonymous.

## What happens after a report
The health and safety team contacts the person who reported within 3 working days. For accidents an accident investigation team is appointed.
