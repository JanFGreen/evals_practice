# Personal protective equipment
Version: 1.2 | Valid from: 2024-05-01 | Status: current

## Footwear
Class S3 safety footwear is mandatory in halls A, B and C.
Footwear is replaced every 12 months, or earlier if it is damaged. You order a replacement from the workwear store.

## Gloves and vests
Protective gloves are mandatory when handling pallets and boxes with sharp edges.
A high-visibility vest is mandatory in loading zones and in the yard.

## Hearing protection
In hall C, in the sorter zone, the noise level exceeds 85 dB. Hearing protectors are mandatory there.

## Back support belts
The company does not issue lumbar support belts (so-called back belts) and does not recommend using them. There is no evidence that they prevent injuries, and they may give a false sense of safety.
