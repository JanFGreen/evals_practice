# Remote work
Version: 2.2 | Valid from: 2025-01-01 | Status: current

## Who it applies to
Remote work is available for office positions. It does not apply to warehouse positions, production positions or drivers.

## Amount
An office employee may work remotely for at most 2 days a week. The days are agreed with the manager and registered as a request in the HRDesk system.

## Benefits
At the start there is a one-off home office equipment allowance of 300 PLN.
For every month in which the employee worked remotely there is a flat allowance of 50 PLN for internet and electricity.

## Information security
When working remotely you use only the company laptop and the VPN connection.
