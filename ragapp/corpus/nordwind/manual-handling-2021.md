# Manual handling of loads
Version: 2.1 | Valid from: 2021-06-01 | Status: withdrawn (replaced by version 3.0 of 2025-03-01)

## Weight limits
One person may lift and carry a load weighing at most 25 kg alone.
Loads heavier than 25 kg are carried as a team (at least two people) or with a trolley.

## Lifting technique
Keep the load close to your body and lift with bent knees.
Avoid twisting your torso while carrying.
