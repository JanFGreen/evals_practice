# Breaks and job rotation
Version: 1.4 | Valid from: 2024-09-01 | Status: current

## Standard break
Every employee whose daily working time is at least 6 hours is entitled to a paid 15-minute break.

## Rotation in the warehouse
At picking and loading stations employees change stations every 2 hours according to the rotation schedule posted in the hall.
Rotation is planned by the shift supervisor. Swapping stations between employees requires the supervisor's approval.

## Working in high temperatures
When the temperature in the hall exceeds 28°C, employees are entitled to an additional 10-minute break in every hour of work.
In such conditions the employer provides unlimited drinking water.

## Screen work
People who work at a screen for at least 4 hours a day have a 5-minute break after every hour of screen work.
