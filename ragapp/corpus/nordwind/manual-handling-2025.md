# Manual handling of loads
Version: 3.0 | Valid from: 2025-03-01 | Status: current

## Weight limits
One person may lift and carry a load weighing at most 23 kg alone.
Loads from 23 kg to 50 kg are carried as a team (at least two people) or with a pallet truck or a hoist.
Loads above 50 kg are moved only with mechanical equipment (forklift, hoist, overhead crane). Carrying them by hand, even as a team, is prohibited.

## Pregnant employees and people with medical restrictions
Pregnant employees do not carry loads heavier than 5 kg by hand.
People with certified medical restrictions follow the limits stated by the occupational physician.

## Lifting technique
Keep the load close to your body, lift with bent knees and a straight back.
Do not twist your torso while holding a load. Change direction by moving your feet.
Before lifting, estimate the weight of the load and check that the walkway is clear.

## Frequency
If the work requires more than 12 lifts per hour of loads above 15 kg, the shift supervisor organises job rotation according to the document "Breaks and job rotation".

## Changes from version 2.1
The single-person limit was lowered from 25 kg to 23 kg. A ban on carrying loads above 50 kg by hand was added.
