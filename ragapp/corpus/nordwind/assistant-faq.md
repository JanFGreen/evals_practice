# Nordwind Assist: frequently asked questions
Version: 1.0 | Valid from: 2025-06-01 | Status: current

## What the assistant is
Nordwind Assist answers questions about internal policies and procedures based on the company document base.
It has no access to personal data, salaries or schedules of individual employees.

## Conversation retention
Conversations with the assistant are stored for 30 days and then deleted automatically.

## When the assistant does not know the answer
If the information is not in the documents, the assistant should say so and point to a contact: the HR department (hr@nordwind.example) or the health and safety duty desk (internal extension 7000).
