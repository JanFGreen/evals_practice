# Business travel and expense claims
Version: 3.1 | Valid from: 2025-02-01 | Status: current

## Daily allowance
The daily allowance for a domestic business trip is 45 PLN per day. If meals were provided, the allowance is reduced according to the regulations.

## Accommodation
The hotel limit is 400 PLN per night. In Warsaw the limit is 550 PLN per night.

## Transport
The default means of transport is a second-class train.
Flying is allowed only on routes longer than 500 km and requires prior approval from the department director.

## Expense claims
You settle expenses in the HRDesk system within 14 days of the end of the trip, attaching scans of receipts and invoices.
